IDEAS FOR ØLKARTET OSLO
========================
Brainstormed in two passes: as a UX designer, then as someone who really
loves beer. Third pass merges and prioritizes. Nothing here is implemented
yet — just a backlog to pick from.


PASS 1 — UX DESIGNER
---------------------
- Marker clustering. 586 bars on one map means the city center (Grünerløkka/
  Grønland/sentrum) is a solid blob of overlapping dots — I hit this myself
  while testing, had to zoom way in to click one bar. Leaflet.markercluster
  (or a simple grid-based cluster) would make the dense area usable and
  still look great zoomed out.
- The color legend says "Billig"/"Dyrt" but no numbers. Now that the color
  scale rescales to whatever's currently filtered/visible, the legend
  should show the live kr values at each end — otherwise "green" and "red"
  are meaningless without opening a popup.
- Colorblind consideration: green-to-red is the single worst gradient for
  red-green colorblindness (~8% of men). Worth either switching to a
  blue→orange scale, or keeping color but adding a redundant cue (marker
  size, or a small price label that appears at higher zoom levels).
- Empty state: if the slider filters out every bar, the map just goes
  blank with no explanation. Add a "no bars match 0 kr – 60 kr, try
  widening the range" message.
- Mobile layout: the floating panel is a fixed-width box anchored
  top-left. On a phone this eats most of the screen. Should collapse into
  a bottom sheet / drawer that can be swiped down to just a handle.
- Persist filter + last map view in localStorage so a returning visitor
  doesn't have to re-set the price range every time.
- Loading & error states for the data fetch itself (currently a failed
  fetch of data/bars.json just shows an empty map with no message).
- A list/table view as an alternative to the map (sortable by price,
  distance, name) — some users just want "give me the 10 cheapest places"
  without panning around a map.


PASS 2 — SOMEONE WHO REALLY LOVES BEER
----------------------------------------
- We already scrape `valid_to` (e.g. "Gyldig frem til kl 23") but never
  show it in the popup! That's the single most beer-relevant fact we're
  sitting on and throwing away — a price that's only valid until 23:00 is
  very different from one that's always valid. Add it to the popup, and
  ideally compute "is this happy-hour price still active right now" using
  the current time vs. valid_to.
- Relatedly, we don't even capture `default_price` (bool: is this the
  everyday price, or a limited-time happy-hour price) from the source
  data. Capturing it lets us badge bars as "🕒 Happy hour" vs "✅ Always
  this price" instead of just a plain number.
- "Round calculator": type in how many friends you're buying for, see the
  total cost of a round at each visible bar. Small input, makes the price
  number feel real.
- Brewery filter — the data already has `brewery` (Ringnes, Carlsberg,
  Budweiser, etc.) per bar. Being loyal to a brand, or wanting to avoid a
  macro lager and find a place with something else on tap, is a real beer
  nerd use case.
- "Surprise me" button — picks a random cheap bar within the current
  filter/view. Good for decision paralysis on a Friday at 17:00.
- Show the Oslo average price as a reference (e.g. a tick mark on the
  slider track at the citywide median) so "110 kr" has context — the
  scraped source data already gives us a city-wide mean/median, we just
  never surfaced it.
- A lightweight "pub crawl" mode: given your location, draw a short
  walking route between the 3-5 cheapest bars within N minutes' walk.
  Bigger feature, but very on-brand for this app.


PASS 3 — MERGED & PRIORITIZED
-------------------------------
Quick wins (small effort, high value):
  1. Show `validTo` in the popup (data already scraped, just not rendered)
  2. Dynamic min/max kr labels on the legend gradient
  3. Empty-state message when the filter matches zero bars
  4. Persist slider range + map view in localStorage

Medium effort:
  5. Marker clustering for the sentrum/Grünerløkka density problem
  6. Capture `default_price` and badge "happy hour" vs "always this price"
  7. Brewery filter (dropdown or chip list)
  8. Mobile bottom-sheet layout for the control panel
  9. Colorblind-safe palette option / redundant price cue

Bigger / stretch:
  10. List view alternative to the map, sortable by price/distance
  11. Round calculator (price × number of friends)
  12. "Surprise me" random-cheap-bar button
  13. Walking pub-crawl route between nearby cheap bars
